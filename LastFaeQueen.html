The Last Fae Queen

Spice level:🌶️🌶️🌶️🌶️🌶️

“Magical bonds, magical orgasms, save the world.”

Kayla had six months left. Six months of rushing from commitment to commitment before she graduated with an engineering degree that would set her up to finally, finally, begin living life. Friends? Romance? Who has time for those things when putting yourself through school?

Everything changed the day she got magic tramp-stamped a few weeks after her 25th birthday. The cute new student in class turned out to be a fae warrior and part of the Queen's Guard, magic turned out to be real, and Kayla was dropped into the middle of a centuries long conflict between Seelie and Unseelie--literally. Now, Kayla has to put her wits and partial engineering degree to the test as the last heir to Encandia, dodging creatures from the Veil hellbent on killing her, ghosts of her trauma haunting her dreams, and far too many suitors to really sort through. Seriously, does each of the Seasonal Realms really need their own consort to the queen?

Whatever. All she needed to do is find four people to sleep with to restore their magic tree and--wait, what you do mean love?

The Last Fae Queen is a RH romantasy with FF, MM, MF, and a sex positive heroine who learns how sex positivity can heal trauma and foster strong relationships.
