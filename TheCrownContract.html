The Crown Contract

Spice level:🌶️🌶️🌶️🌶️🌶️

Mel didn't have many options.

With the little sister she had raised off to live out her dreams at university, the debts Mel had shielded her from are slowly crushing her.

But now, she has a way out. One contract, one signature, and her little sister will be set for life.

All she has to do is secure the Unseelie Crown or die trying.
