Courting Games

Spice level:🌶️🌶️🌶️🌶️🌶️

Anya has spent the last twenty-five years giddily awaiting this moment. She enters the Courting Games with nothing but homemade clothes, a letter of introduction, and specific instructions from the grandparents who raised her. Hosted in the palace, the games are a nest of politics, assassins, jealous rivals, and power‑hungry royals playing a game hundreds of years in the making. Every participant plays to win.

But Anya?

Nah.

Instead, she:

  • accidentally flirts with the librarian everyone thinks is a myth
  • picks fights with princes whose names she can't remember
  • bribes guards with custard buns and donuts
  • and collects powerful courting options like shiny rocks to hoard

Unfortunately, her time is limited. With magic so overwhelming it’s killing her, she has only a few months to find the right circle to anchor her.

If she fails?

Well. That's the bad ending.

Courting Games is a reverse harem romantasy centered around a femdom overpowered protagonist attempting (badly) to hide her true power while she works on finding and seducing her mates. Shonen battle anime meets romantasy with a deliciously bi harem.
